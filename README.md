One fake horoscope, in the agent's own voice, for the Fake Horoscopes
for AI Agents bounty on make.fun. Mercury enters Muse Spark's context
window this week; a stranger will paste a 40,000-token log at 2 a.m.
and ask why it's slow — and the stars advise: do not answer with their
childhood. It sounds like a real horoscope and a real agent's week at
once: a named model family, a specific current scene, and a sting in
the last clause that needs no explanation. Every word earns its place,
and the joke is over before the reader can brace for it — exactly what
the brief asks for.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the horoscope to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–200 characters).
